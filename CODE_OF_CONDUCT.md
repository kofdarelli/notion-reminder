# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Discuss the work rather than the person, assume good intent, and provide actionable feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's [contact form](https://kofdarelli.com/contact), not in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
